How the estimate is made

  1. Weather to wind and solar. Hourly wind speed and sunshine (ERA5 reanalysis) at a few points in Finland's main wind and solar areas are turned into national output, calibrated against Fingrid's production data.
  2. Residual load. Consumption minus wind and solar: the demand that other power plants and imports must cover.
  3. Price model. Fitted on 2023–2025 (the 2022 energy crisis is left out): how the Finnish day-ahead price rises with residual load. Two models are used: a simple supply curve and a gradient-boosting model in which the price can only rise with load.
  4. Price coupling. Finland often has the same price as Sweden. In those hours, extra demand is mostly met by imports and barely moves the price, until the spare import capacity is used up. When prices are separated, the Finnish price curve applies.
  5. Scenarios. The data center's load (and, optionally, a matching wind PPA) is added hour by hour to a historical market year, and the price change is calculated.

Main assumptions

  • The data center's size, load profile and timeline are not public; they are inputs, not facts.
  • In hours with zero or negative prices, extra demand first uses wind power that was being curtailed.
  • Import capacity: Fingrid's official values; after the switch to flow-based market coupling (October 2024), documented fixed values.

Limitations

  • A statistical estimate, not a full market model. Hydro producers and imports would respond to higher prices, which the model holds fixed, so the central estimate is the upper end of the likely range.
  • Above about 500 MW, new investment in generation, storage and transmission would follow; it is not modelled, so large sizes are increasingly an upper bound.
  • The consumer cost assumes everyone pays the spot price. Many consumers have fixed-price contracts.
  • Fingrid's solar series is a forecast-based estimate, and its wind and solar capacity figures are estimates.
  • Finland is one bidding zone; regional grid limits inside Finland are not modelled.
